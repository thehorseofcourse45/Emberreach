# Skill balance update

Standard mode at 1× speed. Best unlocked activity at each level; no XP/speed bonuses, quest rewards or material gathering. Includes failures, stun and node respawn overhead. Farming assumes immediate harvesting/replanting, unlimited seeds and perfect survival on all 15 plots. These are training-time forecasts, not guaranteed calendar completion.

Serial active-skill baseline: 112.1 training days to all 99; 506.1 to all 120. Bonuses shorten these times. Gathering also supplies other skills, so material requirements must be considered as part of an account route rather than blindly added to this sum.

Settlement: homes produce 4 residents per level (maximum 24). Every real-time hourly production tick awards 2,500 XP per resident, plus the normal Township XP multiplier. A developed 24-resident town earns 60,000 base XP/hour: about 9 days to 99 and 72 days to 120, plus construction/setup time. Online and offline ticks share this calculation. An empty town earns no passive XP.

Husbandry: crop harvest XP now scales with crop tier. Existing grow times, seeds, yields and survival rules remain. With only a few manual harvests per day, actual calendar time exceeds the perfect-turnover forecast; it runs alongside your main activity.

Combat retains its damage-based XP formulas. Vitality and Prayer XP accrue alongside attacks; Slayer XP depends on tasks/areas. Supplies and damage output drive their actual leveling times.

The XP curve, existing save XP, levels, item quantities, skill/action IDs and save format remain compatible. This update does not retroactively rescale earned progress.

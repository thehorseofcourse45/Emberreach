1Getting Started

Emberreach is an idle RPG: you pick one activity at a time, and it runs on a timer, ticking away even while you do other things — and while the game is closed. There is no twitch skill. Progress comes from choosing what to train, spending the materials you gather, and steadily upgrading your character.

The one rule of idle games

At any moment you have one active activity — a skill action, or a combat expedition. It repeats automatically until you stop it, you run out of materials, or something dies. Your job is to decide which activity is worth running, keep it supplied, and come back to collect and re-aim.

  1. Start a new journey. You begin at level 1 in every skill with 0 GP (gold).
  2. Gather raw materials. Open Skills → Timbercraft (woodcutting) and start chopping Normal Trees. Logs pile up in your Bank automatically.
  3. Turn materials into gear or XP. Logs feed Firemaking/Cooking; ore feeds Smelting → Smithing. Smith a Bronze Helmet.
  4. Equip and fight. Open Equipment, equip your helmet, then go to Combat and clear the Farmlands.
  5. Spend the rewards. Loot and GP buy upgrades in the shops, unlock buildings in your Settlement, and complete Tasks for bonuses.
  6. Repeat, deeper. Higher skill levels unlock better actions; better gear clears harder areas; harder areas drop better loot. That spiral is the whole game.
Tip: Everything autosaves (every 60 s and on key events). You can close the game at any time — see Offline Progression.

2The Core Loop

Almost all progression is one feedback loop. Internally the self-test calls it the gear loop, and it looks like this:

🌲 Gather→ 🔨 Craft gear→ 🛡️ Equip→ ⚔️ Fight→ 💰 Loot & GP↩

Each pass up the loop raises a ceiling: a higher Mining level lets you mine Runite, which smiths into Runite armour at Forgecraft 85, which lets you survive a God Dungeon, which drops the catalyst to enchant that armour even further. You are never doing just one thing for its own sake — every skill feeds another.

XP & Levels

Each action grants skill XP. Levels run 1→120. Reaching 99 takes 13,034,431 XP; 120 takes 104,273,167. Higher levels unlock stronger actions.

Currencies

GP (gold) is the main currency. Specialist currencies (Slayer Coins, Raid Coins, Stardust, Dream Essence, typed enchant essence) buy things in their own shops.

Modifiers

Every bonus — gear, prayers, potions, pets, mastery, astrology, shop upgrades — is a modifier that stacks into one combined value. More sources = faster, richer, stronger.

4Skills & Mastery

There are 39 skills: 9 combat, 30 non-combat. Every skill levels 1→120 and unlocks better actions as it climbs.

Emberreach uses its own themed names (the familiar term is in brackets). Nothing here is cosmetic — every skill feeds the loop. The nine combat skills train automatically while you fight; the thirty non-combat skills are the activities you pick on the Skills screen (five of them — the signature skills — run their own full systems, detailed in §11).

How an action works

Each action has a level requirement, a base interval (seconds per attempt), a base XP, and outputs (what you get). Start it and it repeats on that interval. Interval and output can be improved by modifiers (gear, mastery, potions…).

Mastery — the per-action progression

On top of your skill level, every individual action builds its own mastery. Mastery brings faster actions, preservation (don't consume the material), and doubling. Each skill also has a shared Mastery Pool that fills as you train, with reward checkpoints at 10%, 25%, 50%, and 95%. Spread training across a skill's actions and you'll bank pool checkpoints that buff the whole skill.

Tip: The Skills screen shows a live ≈ XP/hour and mastery pool % so you can compare actions before committing.

5Gathering Skills

These feed everything else. Start one, walk away, collect a stockpile. (The full level ranges are in §4; this is how they play.)

🪓 Timbercraft

Chop trees for logs. Logs → Firemaking, Cooking fuel, Bowyering.

🎣 Angling

Fish raw fish. Raw fish → Cookery → food that heals in combat.

⛏️ Delving

Mine ore, coal & gems. Ore → Kilncraft (smelt bars) → Forgecraft gear.

🗝️ Larceny

Pickpocket for GP and items. Higher targets risk getting caught (stun).

🏺 Excavation

Dig sites for artefacts to donate to the museum for bonuses.

🔭 Prospecting

Work veins for harvested_essence, a support material.

🔎 Lostfinding

Accept cases, gather trace_evidence, resolve lost caches for rewards.

Nodes & respawns: gathering nodes have HP. Each success chips 1 HP (a preservation roll can skip it); at 0 the node respawns on a timer. You keep working — the game just rotates to the next available node.

7Combat & Expeditions

Combat is still idle — you pick a target and a style, and the fight auto-resolves tick by tick.

Attack styles

⚔️ Melee

Trains Bladework / Might / Warding depending on sub-style. Needs melee weapons & armour.

🏹 Ranged

Trains Marksmanship. Needs a bow/crossbow + ammunition.

✨ Magic

Trains Sorcery. Needs runes (from Runescribing) and a staff.

There's a combat triangle: each style is strong against one and weak against another. Pick the style your target is weak to. Vitality (hitpoints) and Devotion (prayer) level alongside whichever style you use.

Areas vs. dungeons

  • Areas are endless — you fight the same pool of monsters until you Flee or die. Good for farming XP and loot.
  • Dungeons are fixed sequences ending in a boss, often gating a key reward (e.g. enchant catalysts from God Dungeons).

Staying alive

Equip food and it auto-eats when your HP drops. Watch Damage Reduction (DR), evasion, and max hit in the readout. Some areas have hazards that are applied live during the fight. If a fight is a losing battle, hit Flee — or use the Simulator screen first to check your win chance using the real formulas before you commit.

Death is a setback, not a wipe — but you can lose your active run's progress. Simulate hard content first.

8Prayers & Potions

🙏 Prayers

Up to 2 active at once, drawing Prayer Points per minute. 60 prayers grouped by role (accuracy, defence, XP, drops…). When points hit 0 they switch off — the Prayers screen shows time-until-empty. Points come from burying bones (Devotion).

🧪 Potions

One active potion at a time, consumed by charges as its effect triggers. Brewed in Apothecary. Pick the potion that matches your current activity (combat potion for fights, a skilling potion for gathering).

9Gear & Your Bank

Equipment — 14 slots

Weapon, shield/offhand, helmet, body, legs, boots, gloves, cape, amulet, ring, two rings of ammo/consumable, and more. Stats from all equipped items aggregate into your combat profile. Save multiple loadout sets and swap between a combat kit and a skilling kit (ownership is checked when you equip a set).

Equipment slots are indexed internally: 8 = weapon, 9 = shield/offhand, 7 = ring. Equippable tomes from Scribing use the offhand slot.

The Bank (Storage screen)

Items stack and are stored automatically. From Storage you can Search, Sort, Filter, Favourite, Protect (so you don't sell it by accident), Inspect, and act: Sell 1 / Sell All / Equip / Bury (bones → prayer points) / Use. Bank slots have a rising cost curve as you expand.

10Offline Progression & the Action Queue

⏳ The game keeps playing while you're away

When you load a save, Emberreach replays up to 24 hours of elapsed time through the same code that runs live — so offline results match what you'd have earned at the desk. A Welcome-back summary shows what happened: XP gained, items made, levels earned. Your active activity (or queued actions) is what runs while you're gone, so leave something productive running before you close.

🧮 Action Queue

Queue a sequence of skill actions with a policy (e.g. "do X until I have 100, then start Y"). The queue persists in your save and drives both live and offline time, so you don't waste offline hours stuck on a finished task.

Tip: Before a long time away, queue a chain that can't run dry — a gather step that keeps feeding a craft step.

11The Five Signature Skills

Five non-combat skills have their own full systems — they don't use the plain action loop. Each has a pet, mastery-pool effects, a skillcape, potion support, and progression tasks at levels 10/50/99.

🐄 Husbandry (Ranching)

Buy pens and animals, feed them with crops from Farming, and collect produce, meat, and manure. A pen holds two matching animals. Fed animals produce at full speed; hunger decays efficiency down to 25%. With a pair, enough happiness, and 6 hours elapsed, animals breed. Manure is applied before planting to boost one crop.

📜 Scribing (Inscription)

Make paper and ink, research a recipe once (research is finite — it stops after one completion), then scribe texts and equippable tomes. Crafting rolls a quality: Faded, Inked, or Illuminated. Tomes equip in the offhand slot and give a fraction of their crafting XP as a steady supplement. Active glyph effects (haste, doubling, mastery) expire on a shared real-time clock. The skillcape lets you run a second active mastery glyph.

⚙️ Engineering

Craft parts and devices, then install a device in a work slot and point it at an unlocked activity. You start with 2 slots, rising to 6 via level/mastery/cape. Devices run on fuel (coal, later cosmic runes) paid an hour up front; unused prepaid time is saved. Workers use base rates (not your modifiers). The Farmhand auto-collects and replants real plots; a fuelled Scavenger recovers extra drops from monsters you defeat.

🔮 Attunement (Enchanting)

Disenchant spare gear (unequip it to Storage first; protected items are safe) into typed essence — martial, verdant, warding, arcane. Spend essence + runes + gems + boss catalysts to enchant one gear piece: Might (melee), Precision (accuracy), Fortune (doubling), Scholar (XP), Warding (DR). Replacing an enchant overwrites that piece's property — the skillcape allows two. Top-tier enchants need catalysts from God Dungeon bosses, and cleared signature weapons apply Burn or Slow in real combat.

💤 Dreamwalking

Reserve 0–100% of your capped offline time for dreams with a slider. On return you collect Dream Essence, resolve event choices, and spend essence in the Dream Bazaar (temporary XP/doubling buffs, settlement ticks, bundles of paper/coal/manure). Longer dreams gain depth for bigger rewards. Nightmares can nibble a little session essence; mastery mitigates them. A Lucid Draught supercharges one offline dream and is consumed once.

Dreamscapes run from Starlit Meadow (lvl 1) up to Crown of Dreams (lvl 115), each with higher XP and essence per hour.

12Endgame Content

🐉 God Dungeons

The hardest fixed dungeons. Their bosses drop the catalysts that gate top-tier Attunement enchants (Tidebound, Volcanic Infusion).

🏟️ Raid

Wave-by-wave survival with a 3-choice reward pick after each wave. Bank Raid Coins for the Raid Shop.

🗡️ Blight (Slayer)

Take assigned tasks, track kills, earn Slayer rewards, and spend in the Slayer Shop. Some gear is Slayer-locked.

🌑 Abyssal

The deepest combat tier for fully-built characters.

14Ascendancy (Prestige)

Once you've built up enough lifetime XP, the Ascendancy screen lets you reset for a permanent boost: +5% XP and +5% GP per ascension (additive, capped). The screen spells out exactly what resets and what survives before you commit. It's an optional long-term accelerator, not a requirement — only ascend when the reset buys back faster than the progress it costs.

Before you ascend: read the reset/survive list on the screen. Respecialisation isn't available yet, so plan the run you want.

15Quick FAQ

I'm confused — what should I do right now?

Pick a gathering skill, start its first action, and let it stockpile. Then process that material (smith/cook/craft) and equip or sell the result. Clear an easy area for GP. That's the loop — expand it as levels unlock more.

My activity stopped on its own.

You either ran out of input materials, a combat run ended, or a queued step completed. Check the Overview events feed, restock, and restart.

Why is my XP so slow?

Stack more modifiers: better gear, a matching potion, relevant prayers, mastery-pool checkpoints, astrology stars, and pets. Also check you're running the highest unlocked action, not the starter one.

How do I not die in combat?

Equip food (it auto-eats), raise Damage Reduction, pick the attack style the target is weak to, and run the Simulator before hard content.

Can I leave for the night?

Yes — queue a self-sustaining chain or set a Dreamwalking allocation, then close. Up to 24 h replays on load. The Welcome-back summary shows what you earned.

What's the fastest way to power up a single weapon/armour piece?

Level Attunement: disenchant spares for essence, then enchant the piece you actually use (Might/Precision for weapons, Warding for armour).
